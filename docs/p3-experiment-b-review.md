# P3 — Experiment B headless scripted reducer verification

**First-case scope:** `england-school-delivery-v1`, frozen P2 England headline provenance and **authored** six-cell synthetic beneficiary tables. Pure Rust, integer-only, no Hiyal/network/LLM, no provider charges. **Implementation branch:** `feat/p3-pure-reducer` (PR #10). This is a headless game-mechanism acceptance, not scientific validation of England or approval for a persisted multiplayer game. Review before merge.

**Release limits:** The first case specifies *delivery-paid tranche milestones*, not arbitrary new audit-dependent trust-fund/escrow triggers. A promise or ratification does **not** release money; payment is authorized only when already funded work is actually delivered. Reallocation keeps the same scope's partial carry and refuses reducing below pending protected units. No recurring maintenance fee outside its explicit funded service units, no debt or automatic refill. Future extensions must be versioned and should not smuggle in policy effects absent from the authored case.

**P3-07 result:** 72 ratified, 36 rejected by votes and 36 unaffordable. The full tested 36-pair balanced-versus-staff-first comparison yielded **zero strict aggregate weighted-support ranking reversals**. Both aggregate and group-level gains, losses and reversal comparisons, and the tested range, appear in the generated report; zero reversals are not evidence the game is robust outside this authored range. This is no causal or statistical validation.

**Custody boundary into P4:** The present P1 wire checkpoint stores `WorldSnapshot` but not the entire additional P3 `Campaign` state (meeting, command IDs, privately retained effort, audited evidence, historical due shadow scopes, ballots and belief updates). It **must not be used** as a complete persisted P3 checkpoint. P4 must version that private reducer envelope, persist and replay the full state with pack SHA and cursor binding, and ensure that public HTTP projections never serialize `WorldSnapshot` or `ReducerReceipt` truth. P3 completion alone never certifies P4 replay, cross-platform determinism, real LLM behavior or a live provider.

**Ethics and interpretation:** The simulated institution and ministers, fractional work, capacities, responses, political affiliations, belief updates and policy rankings are wholly fictional. Official DfE values support a release-headline scenario only; no real school-beneficiary joint population or policy elasticity was measured. Keep the always-visible fictional-data-twin and nonforecast language in P7.
