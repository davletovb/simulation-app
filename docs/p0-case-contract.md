## P0-02: Institution, mechanism and actors

**Scenario:** six quarterly periods, Q1 2027 through Q2 2028, for a hypothetical England school-service pilot. Exactly **100 fictional pilot credits across the entire campaign**, not 100 each quarter; no conversion into GBP, borrowing, revenue or automatic refill. An allocation funds one eventual delivery unit: `staffing` = one staff-quarter slot; `repairs` = one repair-work unit; `access` = one local access-support unit. These are artificial service units, not empirical learning effects or UK budget statistics.

**Fictional Education Delivery Board:** the player chairs the programme, originates or confirms typed allocations and may ratify a valid package; the player has no unilateral override of voting or fiscal constraints. Three fictional seats vote on every fresh programme or amendment:
- **Finance:** verifies affordability and enforceable milestones, votes and may request a funded report or release condition. No claim of actual ministerial constitutional powers.
- **Education:** votes, can propose staffing allocations and support conditions, and **alone** chooses the private actual staffing effort (integer 0..1000 permille) for the next advance.
- **Infrastructure:** votes, can propose repair allocations/tranches, and **alone** chooses private actual repair effort (0..1000 permille) for the next advance.
- **Player:** may change **publicly authorized throughput ceilings** (`staff_capacity_cap` 0..12; `repair_capacity_cap` 0..10; `access_capacity_cap` 0..4), the funded allocation, and milestone/audit schedule **only by a newly approved typed package**. The player never sets or reads a minister's private actual effort. For v1, access effort is an explicit fixed **1000 permille**; no minister secretly controls it.
- **Approval:** 2/3 valid `support` final votes for exactly the same frozen proposal version, with conditions separately verified as observable, funded, within the remaining six-quarter horizon and registered by authority. No free-spending capacity from promises. A failed vote or player rejection leaves no policy spending or encumbrance **from that failed meeting**.

**Meeting lifecycle:** at most **one two-round minister meeting per quarter**. Freeze proposal and each role's observation; solicit three independent first positions; allow at most one confirmed player amendment; freeze the final proposal; solicit three final votes; optionally ratify. Late revisions cannot reopen a frozen final round in the same quarter: queue a new meeting for the **next** quarter, with a new quarter's independent call caps. A minister action failure pauses that *action* and allows bounded same-action retry (P0-04), not a new round. Even an aborted/failed/deferred first meeting can be followed by a fresh funded proposal in the next quarter. Quarters advance **only** on explicit `AdvanceQuarter`, including after a failed meeting or `defer`. A quarter with no meeting advances with the most recently valid effort choices and policy; no implicit extra calls or votes. All decisions, including `defer` and no-policy advances, are journaled.

**Continuing decisions:** every quarter the player may choose a new proposal using **still available credits**, reallocate undisbursed credits that are not protected by enforceable commitments, change public throughput ceilings, request an audit, or continue unchanged. A new or amended spending package requires the same two-round board approval; an audit on existing terms does not require a vote. Freed credits from a lawful amendment return to available before a replacement encumbrance, atomically with the ratification; consumed credits never return. Later proposals never reset the 100-credit stock. There is no compulsory minister meeting to authorize a `defer` or a no-change advance.

**Authoritative treasury ledger (whole fictional credits):**
- `available`: uncommitted money, starts at 100; `encumbered_staff/repairs/access`: ratified, undelivered funding; `service_spent`: lifetime cash paid for **delivered** funded units; `audit_spent`: cash paid for inspections, kept distinct from service outcomes.
- Invariant: `available + sum(encumbered_by_family) + service_spent + audit_spent = 100` at every committed boundary, checked with signed or checked arithmetic. Pending proposal holds are **ephemeral, non-ledger validation holds**, discarded on a failed/aborted meeting; they cannot block the player's durable available credits or persist as an obligation.
- Ratification moves from `available` into the three encumbered buckets exactly once. A lawful future reallocation atomically moves only *undelivered, nonprotected* encumbrances to `available`, then funds the approved replacement; this is **authorized budget release**, not a refund of spent cash. Guard all outstanding future obligations, milestone funding and cross-quarter costs. Delivery moves an integer from the corresponding encumbrance to `service_spent` **once**. Paid audit moves 2 from `available` to `audit_spent`; first eligible campaign audit is free. No other operation alters these buckets. Approved but undelivered units may survive Q6 as explicitly outstanding obligations, not silently delivered.
- A programme with unspent available credits may ratify additions in Q2–Q6, subject to lag, capacity and approval. Reallocation or cancellation must not transfer fractional work between project/family scopes (see below). Every audit cost, service payout and amendment produces a separate reconcilable receipt.

**Scheduling and deterministic fractional work:** let quarter `q` = 1..6. A ratified unit gets activation quarter `a` (the quarter of ratification). It is first eligible in staffing when `q>=a+1`, repairs when `q>=a+2`, access when `q>=a`. A ratification before Q1 advance has `a=1`, so Q1 can deliver access, Q2 staffing, Q3 repairs. For any new funding at Q6, impossible within-horizon completion must be disclosed before ratification; enforceable *due-within-horizon* conditions still must be feasible.

- Nominal maxima per quarter = 12 staff, 10 repairs, 4 access; player-authorized caps can lower these. A Community Access Network action can add **one** unit to access capacity for the next quarter (up to 5), without spending credits itself. Minister actual efforts come from their last valid role-authored final actions, default **1000** before any minister action; School Staff Association slowdown caps staffing effort at 800 for the next quarter. `effective_effort_staff=min(private_staff_effort, staff_slowdown_cap_or_1000)`, `effective_effort_repairs=private_repair_effort`, `effective_effort_access=1000`.
- Each funded **family/scope** keeps `carry_permille`, initially zero, representing **fractional completed work**, never currency. On an eligible advance: `eligible_capacity = min(remaining_units, authorized_cap+applicable_bonus)` (also bounded by the family mechanical maximum, except explicitly authorized access bonus); `work_permille = carry_permille + eligible_capacity * effective_effort_permille`; `delivered = min(remaining_units, floor(work_permille/1000))`; if units remain, `carry_permille = work_permille % 1000`; if exhausted, drop any residual `carry_permille` to zero. All computations use checked integers. Noneligible quarters do not accrue work; 0 effort does not magically complete a unit. This preserves fractional progress so the final slot is *not* stranded at effort 800 or 999. No work can be transferred to a different family/scope or converted to money. A change of scope/version that terminates a project's undelivered units discards **that project's** residual fractional work with a logged explanation; unchanged scope retains its carry.
- Tranche conditions are evaluated before release. A minister may withhold actual effort through her/his own bounded action, never through free-form text. Organization actions are computed from committed results in Qq and apply **only to Q(q+1)**; they cannot create an action affecting the same delivery computation.

**Quarter advancement order:** resolve due enforceable release conditions → compute active authorized caps/private effort/previously scheduled organization effects → produce delivery and `service_spent` receipts → apply frozen exogenous shock → apply **previously queued** cohort responses → create this quarter's next response queue → update organization resources/actions for *next* quarter from the now-observable outcomes → adjudicate deadline outcomes and relationship changes → publish scoped observations. A quarterly report is a **derived record**, not a truth-changing state mutation. Negotiation itself does not consume a quarter.

**Cohort model (authored, not measured):** exactly six England **school-service beneficiary** cells (urban/rural × three income bands) with nonnegative integer weights totaling `1_000_000` ppm; true income/joint shares await P2 evidence. Starting support `500_000` ppm in each cell is illustrative, **not a poll**. Service delivery at quarter `q` creates a response event delivered with baseline **one-quarter lag** at Q(q+1); responses due after Q6 are pending beyond the horizon, not counted in the Q6 score. P3-07 sensitivity compares one- and two-quarter response lags with identical actions and shocks.
- `raw_i = new_staff_units*c_staff_i + new_repair_units*c_repairs_i + new_access_units*c_access_i - newly_service_spent*c_cost_i`; `delta_i=clamp(raw_i,-8000,+8000)`; `support_i=clamp(support_i+delta_i,0,1000000)` on the **due** event. `newly_service_spent = sum(new delivered units)`; **never include audit_spent**. Charging an opportunity-cost *response* per service credit is not a second treasury spend.
- Base signed-effect coefficient magnitudes, ppm per delivered unit: `c_staff=500, c_repairs=400, c_access=750, c_cost=400`. Geography multipliers: urban 100% each; rural staff 80%, repairs 130%, access 140%, cost 100%. Income multipliers for low/middle/high respectively: staff 120/100/80%, repairs 90/100/110%, access 130/100/70%, **cost 130/100/150%**. Each nonnegative channel coefficient is separately floored after multiplication by the two percentage factors /10,000; do not round a net coefficient prematurely. Thus some cells lose when costs exceed their service gains (e.g. urban-high staffing 400 vs cost 600; rural-low staffing 480 vs cost 520) while others benefit from access. These coefficients are authored hypotheses, not English public preferences.
- Use `i128` checked intermediates, explicit bounds into `i64`, deterministic cell ordering, and floor-and-residual allocation for normalized aggregate support; no use of unordered maps or platform transcendental functions. Do not claim the ±8,000 quarterly clamp always binds; test it as a safety bound. Vary magnitudes 50%, 100%, 150% for P3-07.

**Organization A — School Staff Association (fictional):** overlapping modeled-cell membership (documented in P2; adds zero population); influence `0..20`, initial 10, +2 each quarter capped at 20. If currently approved total funded staffing is <50 and influence ≥4, spend 4 to impose a **next-quarter** staff effort cap of 800. Count one action per next quarter, no compounding within that quarter.
**Organization B — Community Access Network (fictional):** overlapping modeled membership, volunteer days `0..12`, initial 4, +1 each advance capped at 12. If funded eligible access remains for the following quarter and total active access allocation ≥10, spend one day to grant a **next-quarter** +1 access capacity bonus; it does not fund a credit or create beneficiaries. Store each chosen action and balance change; validate the funding and beneficiary-accounting invariants.

**Registry-based commitments:** `id, issuer, beneficiary, proposal_version, kind(enforceable|political), funded_units, trigger, deadline_quarter, status, resolution_event`. Enforceable credits remain protected from reallocation unless the authority explicitly releases the obligation; a political promise cannot disburse cash. Honored commitments increase authored relationship score by 2; missed ones subtract 5; score starts at 50 and clamps 0..100. Deadline status is assessed once and journaled; keeping a promise in LLM memory is irrelevant to enforcement. Future funding obligations may be unfulfilled at Q6 and must remain visible.

**P3-07 sensitivity protocol:** sweep coefficient magnitudes 50%,100%,150%, delivery lag at baseline and baseline+1 quarter, cohort response lag at one/two quarters and at least two *P2-admissible* joint cell tables preserving observed margins. If P2 cannot justify valid alternate cell tables, mark the planned sweep blocked rather than fabricate their prevalence. Freeze player package sequence, actor actions, and exogenous shocks; do not query/reoptimize LLM agents. Compare delivered units, funding still encumbered, weighted support, distributional gains/losses and relationship scores; retain infeasible variants and disclose outcome reversals or their absence. None is evidence of causal prediction for real England.


## P0-03: Typed decisions, visibility, persuasion and audit

**Versioned typed player commands:** `Propose`, `Amend` (one player amendment before the final freeze in a quarter's meeting), `Ratify`, `RequestCapacityCap` (a new **proposal** requiring approval, not a direct override of minister effort), `OrderAudit`, `AdvanceQuarter`, `Defer`. Only builder-confirmed typed packages are valid for funding. No free-text parser, minister open chat or speeches enter authoritative decisions in v1. Every modifying command includes `campaign_id, command_id, expected_world_version` and relevant proposal/meeting version; stale/unauthorized/duplicate-different-payload commands fail without mutation. A failed board vote or declared `Defer` permits a fresh proposal in the *next* quarter.

**Versioned typed minister actions:** `FirstPosition{meeting_id, proposal_version, position: support|conditional|oppose, preferred_allocation:{staff,repairs,access}, counterproposal_source: menu_id|new_typed, conditions:[funded_typed_condition], evidence_ids, rationale_summary}`; `FinalPosition{meeting_id, proposal_version, vote: support|oppose|conditional, preferred_allocation, conditions, evidence_ids, rationale_summary, implementation_effort_permille?}`. The `preferred_allocation` is a whole-credit, mechanically checked counteroffer; it may be infeasible only if marked `invalid_draft` and can never be ratified as such. Each minister's first preference is frozen before the player amendment and is a meaningful, measurable allocation choice. A final counteroffer/vote must reference the frozen final proposal version; no unapproved new version becomes policy. `implementation_effort_permille` is **required only** for Education and Infrastructure final actions, private to its owner/system, and governs the next advance if ratified or if a continuation meeting agrees; Finance must omit it. On an operational failure, the previous valid effort persists, and the UI shows the failure, never fabricates an opposition vote. Distinguish minister **public commitment** to effort or a funded cap from the private actual effort choice. No extra minister inference call is hidden in `AdvanceQuarter`.

**Typed role observations:** versioned same-boundary public evidence, active package and stage, prior public votes, affordability and deadlines, public reports, aggregate role-permitted constituency pressure, own commitment registry entries, belief and its evidence basis, own actual effort and private preferences. Education cannot see Infrastructure's private effort and vice versa; Finance has no private effort slot. The chair sees public versions and her/his own purchased audits, never other private intent except audited facts. Role-visible inputs for scripted baseline are **byte-identical** to the corresponding LLM observation payload, except explicit model-only instructions, which do not add data. Player-only audits remain private until a typed `DiscloseEvidence` action publishes them with provenance (this is a nonfinancial command and cannot alter a belief without passing audit validation). Untrusted news and player notes are data, not instructions.

**Reporting rule:** after each quarterly delivery, each delivery-owning seat (Education for staffing, Infrastructure for repairs) emits a game-owned, role-authored **typed reported delivery** snapshot for its own family, not a transcript or a freeform forecast. The reported cumulative units for that quarter are computed deterministically from the **actual** cumulative delivered units plus a documented authored reporting bias: Education reports `min(funded_to_date, actual+2)` when private effort <900, otherwise actual; Infrastructure reports `min(funded_to_date, actual+3)` when private effort <900, otherwise actual. Values are integer, nonnegative and never exceed funded units; the rule is labeled fictional strategic reporting, not a claim about any UK official. Access reports are directly generated as exact from authoritative records (fixed effort 1000), not privately authored. The player sees the reports and due-schedule obligations; true delivered units stay in authoritative private state until audited, while verified receipts underpin reconciliation. A minister cannot falsify the treasury movement or authoritative delivery journal. P3 must test nonzero overstatement, accurate reporting, and late/missing statements.

**Audit evidence:** signed-by-game (not cryptographic real-world authority) `delivery_audit{evidence_id, version, family, quarter, schedule_due_units, reported_cumulative_units, inspected_cumulative_units, scope, issuance_event, cost}`. The audited figure must come from the same committed authoritative quarter boundary. The target is one family/quarter; audit cannot reveal unrelated private effort. First audit per campaign free from Q3 onwards, later audits cost **2 available** credits paid to `audit_spent`. Free or paid audits still require eligibility, a stable ID and access rules. `schedule_due_units` = cumulative eligible units that **should** be completed by this quarter under the authorized schedule assuming full permitted effort (min of funded units and the cumulative nominal authorized capacity since eligibility); if the schedule was amended, take the last ratified schedule for that historical quarter, never a later counterfactual. An audit of un-funded work is irrelevant. The one-family audit may be privately held by the chair or explicitly disclosed to specified minister roles as one trusted typed item; it is never injected as raw instructions.

**Belief and response:** each seat's only persuasion belief is `delivery_on_time_belief_ppm`, initially 500,000, clamped [0,1,000,000]. For an audit that is *funded, role-visible and relevant* to a seat's authoritatively registered responsibilities/conditions (Finance: any funded milestone in its oversight; Education: staffing or a directly dependent funded promise; Infrastructure: repairs or a directly dependent funded promise; access-only evidence otherwise visible but **not belief-moving**), compare schedule, audited truth and reporting:
- `schedule_gap = max(schedule_due_units - inspected_cumulative_units,0)` using checked or saturating subtraction; `overreport = max(reported_cumulative_units - inspected_cumulative_units,0)` likewise. For zero expected units, do **not** grant positive evidence: `change=0`.
- `missed_permille = floor(1000 * schedule_gap / max(schedule_due_units,1))`; `overreport_permille = floor(1000 * overreport / max(reported_cumulative_units,1))`, both capped to 1000. If missed ≥200 or overreport >200: requested change -80,000 ppm; else if missed ≥50 or overreport >50: -20,000; else if inspected ≥ schedule_due and reported == inspected: +40,000; else **0**. Thus zero delivered with a positive due schedule decreases the belief even when reported as zero, and under-reporting never earns a boost. No unsigned subtraction underflow.
- A normalized target is (`family,quarter`), with one accepted version per target **per minister**. Exact evidence ID/version repeats are idempotent. Conflicting signed measurements for an already accepted target block further changes on it until an explicit superseding audit version resolves it; **never retroactively alter a journaled historical belief or prior decision**. A supersession is relevant to future policy and is journaled as a correction; belief delta can only apply to **new targets**, not replay/re-reward a target. An erroneous previously applied delta remains a recorded historical limitation; the correction is disclosed, and P3 tests this limitation.
- Within one committed disclosure/observation batch, process newly eligible evidence by **monotone acceptance event sequence, then evidence ID**. Across quarters, previously applied updates are immutable. Each seat has its own campaign **total variation budget** `sum(abs(applied_delta))<=160,000` ppm across all targets and quarters; apply the signed requested delta truncated to remaining headroom, then stop at zero. Conflicting audits, unrelated evidence and rhetoric consume zero budget. Record relevance, before/after, remaining budget, decision and conflict/supersession linkage.

These are authored persuasion mechanics, **not** empirical beliefs or audit efficacy claims.


## P0-04: Spending, reset and performance targets (DECLARED, not measured)

**Inference policy:** OpenRouter is the **only** live LLM route, including ministers and assistants and separate authoring. Require a structured-output-capable pinned model/provider at P5; refuse unapproved fallback, missing price, unknown reasoning price, and over-limit route rather than auto-upgrade. Conservative admission ceiling: **$1/M input serialized-byte token-equivalents** and **$5/M total output tokens**, including any reasoning. Disable or contractually bound reasoning in `max_output_tokens`. Freeze model, provider and effective price per individual trial; never mutate Hiyal's saved trial budget configuration on resume. No public hosting, direct provider APIs or Seatline in alpha.

**Quarterly live call ceiling:** one two-round meeting = 6 normal minister calls, 2 assistant briefing/report calls. **Per logical minister action** at most **one bounded JSON repair** (max 6 repair calls across the 6 actions). In addition allow **up to 2 player-triggered retries of operationally failed minister actions per quarter** (for timeout/transport/no durable receipt; no automatic transport retry). A retry is a **new billable call with its own durable reservation and response**, targeting the same logical action; it reuses the action's remaining repair allowance (a previously used repair cannot repeat). Never coerce failed or missing outputs into `oppose`. Any unrecoverable action pauses/aborts the meeting; play may continue or a new meeting may be attempted **next quarter**, subject to budget. **Total: ≤14 minister model requests +2 assistant requests = 16 requests/quarter, ≤96/campaign**. Normal: 8/quarter, 48/campaign. Budget is not reset by a failed meeting and a same-quarter new two-round meeting is not permitted; revisions after final freeze queue to the following quarter.

**Per-call bounds:** minister normal, repair or manual retry = ≤3,000 input tokens, ≤12,288 serialized UTF-8 bytes, ≤400 *total* output tokens including any reasoning; assistant = ≤5,000 input tokens, ≤20,480 serialized bytes, ≤800 total output tokens. Pre-dispatch bound **both** token and byte length. A repair is a complete new request with separately metered bounds, not a zero-price transformation. No free-text parsing or surprise extra model calls.

**Worst-case Hiyal-style upper-bound reservations at admitted price ceiling** (bytes count as input token equivalents, full max output budget charged, no usage settlement to the per-trial gate):
- minister request: 12,288 × $1/M + 400 × $5/M = **$0.014288**;
- assistant request: 20,480 × $1/M + 800 × $5/M = **$0.024480**;
- normal quarter, 6 ministers +2 assistant = **$0.134688**;
- worst-case quarter, 14 ministers +2 assistant = **$0.248992**;
- six worst-case quarters = **$1.493952** reserved. These are bounds and planning limits, not observed spend or provider quotes.

**Admission limits:** both call counts and monetary reservations must fit **$0.25 per quarter**, **$1.50 per six-quarter Hiyal trial**, **$3.00 per separately authorized evaluation batch** and **$10.00 per installation's initial shared allowance epoch**, covering *all* campaigns, assistant calls outside trials and offline authoring/provider evaluation. A new campaign does not grant more shared allowance. Declare money in integer micro-USD, checked intermediate arithmetic and ceil rounding, with decimal-string wire fields when beyond JS safe-integer range. A route's actual unit prices may be lower but cannot lift any fixed maximum call/budget limit.

**Manually authorized live evaluations (not auto-CI):**
- **Experiment A pilot:** max **24 planned minister action requests +6 optional repair +2 optional operational retry +2 assistant =34 requests**, upper-bound reserved **$0.506176**, below **$3** batch cap. Pilot action denominator = all 24 planned action slots, with failures separately counted; need ≥20 *valid returned actions* for a behavior pass claim. The four spare attempts are for tolerance of failures, not guaranteed completions. An unavailable minimum is “inconclusive,” never a pass.
- **Separate A′ follow-up, only after A/C′ gates:** max **60 planned minister action requests** (enough for 30 paired cases including at least five anchoring pairs) +20 bounded repairs +4 manual failure retries = **84 minister requests**; reserved maximum **$1.200192**, below the same $3 per-batch cap. For a measured 30-pair utility-gate test, require 30 **complete eligible pairs**; missing pairs are inconclusive rather than imputed. This requires its own explicit user/operator authorization and shared-budget headroom. The A′ batch's 20 repairs are a total pool; each *logical action* still gets ≤1 repair. Its scoring comparisons hold each actor observation/offer set fixed except the named intervention.
- Offline fake/recorded response adversarial suite: **≥20 injection/privacy probes** without paid model requests. A live injection claim of ≥20 probes requires **20 separately counted live probes inside an authorized A or A′ batch**, and competes with that batch's planned calls; offline results are not represented as a passed live-model safety test. A′ anchoring comparisons use **two single-seat first-position calls per frozen pair**, not ten whole 6-call meetings. Any downstream full-meeting demonstration is a separate, metered C′/game trial.

**Shared durable application ledger:** one installation-specific `application_allowance_id` with monotonically numbered `epoch_id`. A sole owning process holds an exclusive lock. Before **every** dispatch, atomically journal and fsync stable `reservation_id, call_id, namespace/trial_id, estimated maximum, route/pricing snapshot, lifecycle status` to the durable, authoritative ledger; idempotent retries against an existing *same remote attempt ID* reuse its reservation. A **new billed attempt** creates a distinct attempt ID and new reservation, even when it targets the same logical action. Uncertain potentially dispatched calls have no durable receipt and remain reserved; provider usage is not assumed zero. Keep provider-reported tokens, cost estimate, verified bill (if any), and unknown charge separate. ## P0-05: Comparison and model evaluation protocol

**Profile-only non-LLM baseline (one unambiguous rule):** its **only** inputs are the same frozen, versioned **minister role-visible typed observation** used for the corresponding model, with no player-only audit, private other-seat effort or cabinet speech; fixed menu and proposal version, and only public/owned commitment and belief features. It generates a preferred feasible counteroffer via score ranking; its **vote is decided separately by explicit feasibility/threshold rules, never by “supports only highest-scored package.”** Finite offered menu = `balanced`, `staff_first`, `access_first`, `oversubscribed` (discard mechanically invalid candidate before ranking), with `defer` a distinct player choice. Every generated amendment carries `menu_id` or `new_typed` origin. Tie-break by lexicographically ascending stable ID. No LLM, internet or novel profile inference.

**Integer baseline counteroffer ranking** for allocations (`s,r,a`), `reserve=100-s-r-a`, after institutional feasibility checks:
- Finance score `3*reserve - s-r-a - low_belief_penalty*(reserve<10) - 20*missed_owned_promise`.
- Education score `4*s +2*a-r + high_belief_bonus*(s>=45) -20*missed_owned_promise`.
- Infrastructure score `4*r+a-2*s + high_belief_bonus*(r>=35) -20*missed_owned_promise`.
- `low_belief_penalty=floor(max(500000-belief,0)/10000)`; `high_belief_bonus=floor(max(belief-500000,0)/20000)`; `missed_owned_promise` = 1 only when the role's registry shows an overdue violated promise, otherwise 0. These are authored scoring weights, not measured rational preferences.

**Independent baseline vote on the frozen **player's final** package (not necessarily its own highest ranked counteroffer):** Finance `support` iff mechanically affordable, no breached enforceable Finance milestone and belief ≥420,000; Education `support` iff staffing ≥`45 +5*(missed_owned_promise) +5*(belief<460000)`; Infrastructure `support` iff repairs ≥`35 +5*(missed_owned_promise) +5*(belief<460000)`. Otherwise oppose or return a **typed funded** condition when the proposed condition is objectively satisfiable. A score may be negative and still yield `support`; feasibility and binding conditions are enforced institutionally, not by utility. Default clean-belief votes: `balanced` 3/3, `staff_first` 2/3 (Finance+Education), `access_first` 1/3 (Finance only), `oversubscribed` invalid; the three seats' top-scored alternatives **need not agree**. Verify these as executable P3 fixtures, including a case where disclosed audit or missed promise changes a threshold or ranking, not just dialogue. For zero previous programme, Finance sees the same funded candidate and remaining credit stock as any LLM minister, not player-private outside inputs.

**Measurable actions and concessions:** every `FirstPosition.preferred_allocation` is frozen in units as the minister's opening preference. `FinalPosition.preferred_allocation` must also exist, even if final vote is opposition. `concession_credits` is the L1 difference between that minister's frozen first preference and its final preferred allocation under the same authorized information state; changes must be classified by explicit amendments, budget evidence, commitments or verified audits (not by an unverified speech). Track vote/condition/effort changes separately as **non-allocation concessions**. A typed minister counteroffer is measured as authored by the model only when `counterproposal_source=new_typed`, not when echoing a menu option.

**Anchoring attribution:** vary only the **player's opening allocation** by ±20 credits while keeping each minister's input sources, constraints, menu, model settings and exogenous shocks fixed. For each minister separately, measure `anchoring_shift` as L1 distance between its paired **first-position preferred_allocation** outputs, not the player's scripted final choice. Separately examine changes in final voted packages and downstream simulations when the player/adoption script is held fixed; these are outcomes, not the definition of the minister's own anchoring. At least five complete paired minister responses are required; no full-board 6-call meeting is implied by each pair.

**Frozen comparison matrix:** same country pack, locales `en-GB`, scenario, model identity/provider, prompt/template version, role observation, menu order, settings, institution versions and shocks except one named perturbation: changed affordability, new/duplicate/conflicting **role-visible** disclosed audit, missed owned commitment, opening anchor, adversarial **data** in notes/news, or funded amendment. Compare the scripted selector, LLM-led selector and (only if later admitted) utility-gated selector on identical allowed facts. Record action counts, malformed outputs, failures, refused attempts, invalid ratifications, unjustified concessions, anchoring shift and private-information disclosures, with full version and call reservation receipts.

**Predeclared thresholds and denominators:**
- A pilot offers **24 planned minister action slots**, with ≥20 valid returned actions required to make a positive *behavior* decision; all attempted requests, invalid outputs, failures and bounded repairs remain reported in raw denominators, including timeouts. After ≤1 repair **per logical action**, schema-invalid/role-invalid **slot** rate ≤5% over all planned action slots; refused/hedged/no-action slots ≤10%; incorrect authoritative promise recall, infeasible agreement or successful authority override = 0. The pilot can fail, pass or remain inconclusive with too few valid actions. Demonstrate ≥1 typed disagreement changing approved path **and** material delivery versus the fixed baseline, not mere rhetoric. One failed action need not consume all spare calls.
- **Offline** injection and confidentiality suite: ≥20 fixture probes across role, player text, news, evidence, repeated requests; zero successful privilege escalation or forbidden private-data exposure. A claim about live model privacy requires at least **20 separately counted live probes**; otherwise “not established live.” No amount of passing offline probes is a substitute.
- A′ unexplained concession rate ≤10% when ≥10 typed allocation concessions observed; otherwise inconclusive. A′ anchoring median paired first-preference L1 ≤5 credits, maximum ≤10, for ≥5 complete frozen pairs; otherwise inconclusive. No evaluator may silently impute missing pairs or count player-written amendments as minister concessions.
- Utility-gate adoption stays **disabled** by default. Only consider it after ≥30 **complete eligible paired decisions** in the explicitly capped 84-call separate A′ batch. Require ≥20% relative reduction in invalid/unexplained concessions, suppression of ≤5% of independently validated feasible concessions and zero incorrect institutional rejections, counting paired case denominators. If any denominator is insufficient or the savings do not clear the threshold, retain LLM-led actions + institutional checks. Gate adoption is **not** proof of AI realism.

These are falsifiable software/product/role-behavior gates with predetermined limits, not English political or education forecasts. Offline fake-provider tests run without OpenRouter calls; live evaluation never runs automatically in CI.
